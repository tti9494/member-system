4. **물리 블록**: 씬 지배 물질의 물리 기반 모션 지문을 매 썬 명시한다 — S01 **대문 개방과 한기**(the great doors swinging wide with real hinge weight, breath fogging in the freezing air, robes stirring in the draught), S02 **어전의 정지**(the king leaning forward with slow deliberate weight, the ranked officials motionless as carved figures, the cold light lying level), S03 **얼굴의 균열**(the jaw tightening in tiny increments, the beard trembling once, the composure cracking in stillness — no melodramatic gesture), S04 **문서의 하중**(the document carried flat on both hands with real paper weight, the binding cord swinging once, the seal mark catching the cold light), S05 **낙하와 프리즈**(the document falling with real weight, the frozen instant of impact, fine dust hanging motionless in the light — the impact sound is added in post-production), S06 **메워지는 어전**(the palace guards filing in with measured steps, the crimson robe swinging as he shouts, the ring of guards closing without running), S07 **거두어지는 팔**(the guards taking his arms with unhurried firm grip, the gache hairpins dead still, the court exhaling once), S08 **칼날 빛과 먼지**(the knife of light trembling almost not at all, the paper grain sharp, the darkness pressing in).
5. **세이프 필터(정본 AG 준수)**: 유혈·상해 일체 없음 — **blood·wound·gore 동반 금지**. 체포는 무력 행사 없이 팔을 거두는 수위 행동으로만 렌더, 검은 허용 어휘는 `sheathed swords` 한정. 절규·과장 고통 연기 배제 — 윤경헌의 S06 외침은 정본 대사의 격렬함이지 영상 폭력이 아니다. 전 등장 인물 성인 — `all characters are adults`.

8. **문자 슬롭 방지(정본 AG ①)**: 원본 문서·밀서의 판독 가능한 글자 금지 — 문서는 `dense vertical columns of brush-written text rendered only as illegible calligraphic texture, no readable letters, no characters, no labels`(S04·S05), 밀서는 `the seal mark on it intact, no readable writing anywhere on the page`(S08). 어전 편액 등 건축 한자는 클린 프레이밍 가드가 전면 차단한다.
